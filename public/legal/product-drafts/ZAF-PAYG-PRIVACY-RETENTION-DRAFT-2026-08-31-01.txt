2. Draft purpose and lawful-basis analysis

Owner and legal reviewer must confirm the Article 6 basis for each purpose. The working proposal is contract necessity for forming and administering the booking, sending essential confirmation/cancellation information and handling the payment; legitimate interests, subject to a recorded balancing assessment, for proportionate fraud prevention, capacity integrity, safety administration and defence of legal claims; and legal obligation where a record is required by tax, accounting, consumer, safeguarding or other law.

Date of birth, attendance or workout context can sometimes reveal or be combined with health information. Do not treat a generic legitimate-interest label as permission to collect health data. If special-category data is genuinely required, document both an Article 6 basis and an Article 9 condition and provide a just-in-time notice before collection.
