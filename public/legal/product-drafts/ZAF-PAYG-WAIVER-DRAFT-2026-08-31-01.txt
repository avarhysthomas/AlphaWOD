1. Who is signing

This acknowledgement is for the adult named attendee buying the specific PAYG class. The signer confirms that they are the named attendee and are aged 18 or over. Another person cannot sign this acknowledgement for an adult attendee through the public PAYG flow.

2. Nature of the activity

The class may involve strenuous exercise, strength and conditioning, functional movements, running, jumping, lifting, use of free weights or machines, repeated effort and coached group activity. Physical activity carries inherent risks including slips, trips, falls, collision, overexertion, muscle or joint injury, aggravation of an existing condition and, rarely, serious injury.

Zero Alpha Fitness must take reasonable care and provide services with reasonable care and skill. This acknowledgement records informed participation; it is not a promise that an attendee will never be injured and it does not transfer to the attendee a risk caused by Zero Alpha Fitness's negligence.

3. Attendee responsibilities

The attendee agrees to:

• follow reasonable coaching, safety and equipment instructions;

• use equipment only as instructed and wear suitable clothing and footwear;

• work within their ability and stop immediately if they feel pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath or unsafe;

• tell a coach promptly about an immediate concern relevant to safe participation; and

• not participate while impaired by alcohol, illegal drugs or any condition that makes participation unsafe.

The public PAYG form should not invite medical history or diagnosis in a general text field. If an attendee needs a reasonable adjustment or needs to share information for immediate safety, they should contact support@zeroalphafitness.co.uk before the class so Zero Alpha Fitness can provide an appropriate secure route and decide what can safely be offered. This is not a substitute for medical advice.

4. Emergency response

If the attendee becomes ill or injured, staff may provide reasonable first aid, contact emergency services and use or disclose the minimum necessary information for immediate care, safety and incident management. The attendee remains responsible for costs charged directly by an external healthcare or emergency provider where lawful.

5. Rights and responsibility

Nothing in this acknowledgement excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, breach of statutory rights, or any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

So far as fair and lawful, Zero Alpha Fitness is not responsible for harm caused by an attendee's deliberate unsafe conduct, material breach of clear safety instructions, or deliberate concealment of information that the attendee knew made participation immediately unsafe. Any question of responsibility depends on the facts and applicable law.
